# Attribution and derived distribution

Shnitzel's own skills and collectors are MIT licensed. Pstack originates with Lauren Tan in cursor/plugins (MIT). The cross-runtime port is michael-denyer/pstack-claude, with its own declared port adaptations and third-party notices.

The updater downloads pristine exact-revision sources and builds a derived local engine. It applies original-upstream changes through the port's substitution and three-way merge machinery. This distribution therefore uses and adapts upstream content; it is not accurately described as copying none of it. Engine LICENSE, NOTICE and bundled third-party attribution files are retained. Exact sources, included changes, runtime exclusions and adaptation records are in the engine manifest and tracked upstream lock.
